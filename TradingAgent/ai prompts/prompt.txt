===============================================================================
 PART 3 — KNOWN ISSUES & GAPS (to fix next)
===============================================================================

1. Non-RL strategies (Random, RuleBased SMA) don't work correctly
   - The SMA calculation in the Python strategy is approximate, not real
   - Signal detection is unreliable

2. Agent cannot choose its own stock
   - Currently hardcoded to trade one symbol
   - Should scan all 10+ symbols and pick the best opportunity

3. Position size is fixed
   - Always trades 10 shares regardless of confidence, volatility, or risk
   - Should use dynamic sizing (Kelly Criterion, volatility-adjusted)

4. No profit-driven decision logic
   - Decisions are purely signal-based
   - Should use expected value with win rate + signal strength
